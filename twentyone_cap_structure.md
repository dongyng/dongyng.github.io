### 3.7 Unverifiable / Ambiguous Items
1. **RSU grant date ambiguity**: Employment agreements promise RSU grants "as soon as practicable following the Closing Date and after the Company becomes eligible to file a registration statement on Form S-8." The S-8 was filed April 1, 2026. Whether RSUs were considered "granted" before then for dilution purposes is a legal/accounting question. I treat them as dilutive from the date of public disclosure (S-1, Jan 5, 2026).

2. **CFO option intermediate state**: Between Dec 8, 2025 and Jan 2, 2026, the CFO option was 941,620 shares. The amended award (970,201) replaced it. For nodes between Dec 15 and Jan 1, the lower number applies.

3. **Convertible Notes "$46.3M additional"**: The S-1 states total $486.5M = $340.2M (Subscription) + $100.0M (Option) + remainder. This remainder appears to include the Sponsor's subscription and possibly additional allocations. The exact breakdown of the $46.3M is not separately itemized in the filings reviewed.

4. **Potential redemption reversals**: Like most SPACs, some shareholders may have redeemed before the merger vote. The final Class A share count (346,548,153) reflects whatever redemptions occurred. Pre-close CEP filings showed the full 10M public shares in trust, but the final number depended on the redemption rate at the merger vote.

5. **No anti-dilution adjustments triggered**: Through July 1, 2026, the Class A share count has remained at 346,548,153. The convertible note conversion rate (72.0841/$1000) has standard anti-dilution provisions but none appear to have been triggered.

6. **Class B non-economic**: Class B shares (304,842,759) carry voting rights only — no economic interest, no dividends, non-transferable. They should NOT be included in a fully-diluted economic share count. The Company's own BPS (Bitcoin Per Share) metric was refined to use only Class A shares, confirming this treatment.
