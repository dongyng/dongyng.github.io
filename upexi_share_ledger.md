**Residual explanation (−386,721 Feb base → Mar 31)**:
The residual of −386,721 arises because:
- The base of 69,760,581 likely INCLUDES the Feb 9 RDO shares (6,337,000)
- BUT the base date (Feb 9) is a single day, while the February buyback (1,637,500) is a full-month aggregate
- If buybacks were NOT evenly distributed — e.g., concentrated after the RDO closing — the actual buyback between Feb 9–28 could be close to the full 1,637,500 (not pro-rated)
- Using full Feb buyback: 69,760,581 − 1,637,500 − 840,561 = 67,282,520 vs actual 66,895,799 = gap of −386,721
- This gap likely reflects: (a) additional buybacks outside the monthly table (e.g., shares surrendered for tax withholding on RSU vesting), (b) small warrant exercises adding shares partially offsetting buybacks, or (c) the base including the RDO shares but the RDO closing being AFTER the "as of" determination

**For conservative modeling**: treat the −386,721 as additional undated buyback/retirement.

**Δ Mar 31 → Apr 20 (+20,495)**:
Small net increase, likely RSU vesting minus minor buybacks or ATM activity.

## NOTES

### 1. ATM Program
- The ATM program's existence is confirmed by the XBRL context `AtTheMarketOfferingProgramMember` and the subsequent-event disclosure of 3,345,534 shares sold Apr–May 2026.
- The initial ATM capacity and total shares sold before April 2026 are NOT explicitly stated in the filings reviewed. No 424B5 prospectus supplements specifically labeled "ATM" were found in the Feb–Jun 2026 window beyond the Feb 9 RDO prospectus.
- The exact ATM sales agreement (e.g., with which agent, maximum capacity) could not be isolated — it may be referenced in the FY3Q26 10-Q "Liquidity and Capital Resources" section which was not fully extracted.
- Q2 FY26 (Oct–Dec 2025) had NO ATM sales in the buyback table. If ATM sales occurred in Q1 FY26 (Jul–Sep 2025), they would appear in the equity roll-forward.
