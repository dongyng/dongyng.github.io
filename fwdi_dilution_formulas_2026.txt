   not individually disclosed before May 14. Additionally, ~605,000 new shares
   were issued (5,000 option exercises + 600,000 'other') in Q1 2026. The net
   of ALL activity through Mar 31 was 76,314,617 outstanding, but this was not
   known until the May 14 10-Q. The Apr 15/May 1 formulas are therefore
   approximate.

2. Pre-Funded Warrants at Apr 15/May 1: The 600,000 exercise was not known
   until the May 14 10-Q. The formulas use 12,864,602 for these nodes.

3. $0.01 warrants: The May 18 Form 4 shows a distribution of 4,458,796 warrants
   from a pooled vehicle to an individual. This is a change in BENEFICIAL
   OWNERSHIP, not an exercise. However, without the Q2 2026 10-Q (not yet filed
   through Jul 1), we cannot confirm definitively that no $0.01 warrants were
   exercised. The anti-dilutive table in the next 10-Q should be checked.

4. 200,000 management options: The 10-Q reports 896,606 management options
   granted in Q1 2026, but the baseline formula only totals 696,606 (323,303 +
   323,303 + 50,000). The remaining ~200,000 options may have been granted to
   other employees after Mar 15 or may have different terms not captured in
   the formula. The grant date fair value is $3.07/share, suggesting low
   strike prices not material to dilution calculation at most price levels.

5. "7.55M-SOL purchase financing": No SEC filing referencing this was found
   through Jul 1, 2026. The Galaxy $80M loan facility is non-convertible
   senior secured debt. If this refers to an equity raise, it had not been
   disclosed in SEC filings by Jul 1.

6. Next 10-Q: Expected for the period ending Jun 30, 2026, to be filed ~Aug 2026.
   Will provide updated common shares outstanding, warrant reserves, and
   option activity for Q2 2026.
