4. THE BASELINE FORMULA ERROR:
   The baseline formula uses Common OS = 672,951,662 (from POS AM dated Dec 4, 2025).
   HOWEVER, this number is the "total shares outstanding" denominator used in the
   Selling Stockholders table, which INCLUDES the pre-funded warrant shares (133.1M)
   and convertible note shares (100M) as if they were common stock (per SEC convention
   for the ownership percentage calculation when these securities are being registered
   for resale).

   VERIFICATION: 439,850,889 (actual common per Nov 14 share count update)
                + 133,100,773 (PFW underlying shares registered)
                + 100,000,000 (CD shares registered)
                = 672,951,662 ✓

   The ACTUAL common shares outstanding as of Dec 4, 2025 was ~439.9M (per the Nov 14
   share count update in the Q3 10-Q, which is the closest hard disclosure).

   The baseline formula therefore DOUBLE-COUNTS the PFW shares by:
   (a) including them in the 672.95M common count, AND
   (b) adding them again via the treasury-stock method (71.705M @ $0.001).

   CORRECTED approach for Dec 4, 2025:
     Common OS = ~439.851M (from Nov 14 share count)
     PFW = 71.705M outstanding
     $6.33 warrants = 0.589M (588,501)
     CDB = 0 (repaid Sep 29)
