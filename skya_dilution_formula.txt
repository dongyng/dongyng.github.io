CAVEATS & NOTES
================================================================================

1. DATE MISMATCH: The 10-K (filed Mar 31) reports common shares as of Mar 24
   but warrants/options only as of Dec 31.  The 10-Q (filed May 14) reports
   common as of May 8 but warrants/options only as of Mar 31.  At EVERY
   reference date, the common count is temporally AHEAD of the warrant count,
   causing double-counting of exercised shares.  The "Estimated Consistent
   Formulas" section above provides adjusted figures.

2. PRE-FUNDED & STRATEGIC ADVISOR = DE-FACTO COMMON: Both have $0.0001
   strike.  The company itself includes them in BASIC EPS (10-K p.F-12,
   10-Q p.F-18).  For any DS25 >> $0.0001, the treasury method yields
   essentially 100% of these as incremental shares.

3. BASELINE ERRATA:
   - Strategic advisor: 6,013,675 → 6,321,367 (corrected in 10-K)
   - Pre-funded at Dec 31: 37,717,312 (not 37,371,158)
   - Repriced warrants: 328,196 → 12,391 remaining (rest now common)
   - Options: 1,785,495 was only the $6.41 bucket; total is 1,875,482
     with wavg strike $9.76, not $6.41
   - The baseline omitted Series B warrants (5,307 @ $0.292) which are
     deep ITM and material at most price levels

4. STAPLED WARRANTS: 63,213,672 at $9.75.  NO exercises detected in any
   filing (unchanged from Dec 31 through Mar 31).  The stock price needs
   to exceed $9.75 for these to be ITM.

5. NO NEW INSTRUMENTS: Zero new dilutive securities issued between Jan 27
   and Jul 1, 2026, based on all 8-Ks, 10-Q, and ownership filings reviewed.

6. ATM: $236.6M shelf active but ZERO sales in Q1 2026.  No data on Q2
   sales (next 10-Q would cover this).

7. UNVERIFIABLE: Post-May-8 common share count.  Pre-funded/SA warrant

   Any option grants between Mar 31 and Jul 1 (employment agreements
